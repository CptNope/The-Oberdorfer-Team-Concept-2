# Surface brief — The Oberdorfer Team, Concept 2 (site + brand book)

Scope: homepage (Persuade) as the lead surface; search (Operate), property, community, agents, Field Guide (Read) inherit the same world. Brand book (Read) documents it.
Audience/job: buyers, sellers, relocators, explorers in Central MA; find homes, understand towns, reach a specific person.
Proof on hand: none beyond names (Brandon & Kait Oberdorfer, REWAP Brokerage LLC). All listings, bios, prices, contact data synthetic and labeled. Photography = art-directed frames with shot direction (user decision).
Constraints: REWAP attribution configurable and subordinate; vendor-neutral IDX; fair-housing-neutral discovery; WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Central Massachusetts as a surveyed quadrangle sheet. Place is framed the way a USGS quad frames ground: neatline, collar, legend, adjoining sheets. Refuses hero-photo + search bar + card grid + testimonial strip.

OWN-WORLD: Quad paper ground (cool near-white, faint green-grey), contour clay linework, woodland forest as the drenched field color, water blue for links/info, oxblood reserved for primary routes/actions. Besley (county-atlas Clarendon) voice; Overpass (highway-sign gothic) for UI and data; map-lettering conventions: towns in widely spaced roman caps, water/landform italics. Neatline frames with coordinate ticks; collar credit block carries brokerage attribution; legends are filters; adjoining-town names in page margins are navigation.

STORY: The visitor sees the region as a living map, understands the team reads this ground personally, picks a path (Buying, Selling, Relocating, Exploring, Advice) from the legend, and lands on a named person with a contextual next step.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-viewport sheet inside a neatline with corner coordinates (42°16′N 71°48′W). Generated contour terrain fills the field; six towns lettered in spaced caps at schematic positions. "Find / your / place." in Besley at the 6rem display ceiling, left-weighted over the terrain. Lower-right legend block lists the five intent paths as the primary actions; collar strip below: "The Oberdorfer Team · Brokered by REWAP Brokerage LLC". Hovering/focusing a town lifts its contour hill and opens its place frame.

FORM: Topographic quadrangle sheet; position 1 on my ordered list (user chose Impeccable's pick over the assigned Wayfinding direction, #6); seed key c6b1583b (degraded roll, no challengers). Signature interaction: contour draw-in + town hover lift; journeys drawn as elevation profiles; map-to-story view transitions.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
