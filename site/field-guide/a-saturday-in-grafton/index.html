A Saturday in Grafton

A morning on the common, a walk in the woods and the case for a town organized into villages.

Around hereKait OberdorferPartnerAugust 13, 2026

If you're trying to understand Grafton, start at the common. It's the old center of town, on a hill, surrounded by churches and houses that have been painted white for a very long time.

From there, go walk. Hassanamesit Woods has trails through conservation land with a long history, and the Willard House & Clock Museum preserves the homestead of a family of clockmakers. Then drive down into the villages and notice how different each one feels.

That's the point of Grafton: it isn't one place. If you're house-hunting here, the village matters as much as the house.
