Worcester

New England's second-largest city, built on hills, mills and three-deckers, and still remaking itself one block at a time.

Shrewsbury

A town on the east shore of Lake Quinsigamond, with a historic center and neighborhoods that climb away from the water.

Grafton

A town of villages: a classic common on the hill, mill villages along the water, and a commuter-rail station in the north.

Holden

Higher ground northwest of the city: woods, reservoirs, stone walls and a compact town center.

Auburn

Where the highways meet, and where a professor launched the first liquid-fueled rocket from a cabbage field in 1926.

Millbury

A Blackstone River town with mill history, a hilltop mansion and one of the region's key highway interchanges.
