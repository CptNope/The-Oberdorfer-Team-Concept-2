# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS for the concept, published on GitHub Pages (repo CptNope/The-Oberdorfer-Team-Concept-2). Content is modeled as JSON (agents, communities, properties, guides, market updates, brokerage settings) so it maps 1:1 to the production target: a WordPress FSE block theme with custom post types, taxonomies and relationships, hosted on Jeremy's Cloudways infrastructure. Fonts self-hosted. No framework, no build step.

## Users

- **Buyers** in or moving to Central Massachusetts (Worcester County), browsing on phones in spare moments and on laptops when serious; they want to find homes fast and understand towns they don't know yet.
- **Sellers** who own homes in the area and want to understand value, preparation and timing without being pushed into a funnel.
- **Relocators** who know the region only by name and need orientation: towns, commute, housing character.
- **Explorers / future clients** who read local content long before they transact.
- Secondary: Brandon & Kait Oberdorfer and future team agents, who need pages that represent them personally and route leads to them.

## Product Purpose

The digital home of The Oberdorfer Team, a Central Massachusetts real-estate team operating under REWAP Brokerage LLC. It must work as a polished property-search platform and as an editorial, locally knowledgeable presence that begins a conversation with a real person. Success: visitors understand who the team is, trust their local knowledge, enjoy exploring homes and towns, and know exactly whom to contact.

## Positioning

A team-owned, personally handled local practice: place (home, street, town, stage of life) understood through local knowledge and personal guidance, rather than a listings database with a contact form. The team owns its brand, domain, content and SEO independent of the brokerage's site and technology.

## Operating Context

- Brokerage affiliation: REWAP Brokerage LLC (Worcester, MA). Principal broker is an attorney; the Oberdorfers operate as a team under it. REWAP is shown typographically only (no logo use required) and must never dominate, but the team must never appear to be an independent brokerage. Exact compliance wording must be configurable in one place.
- Previous site was broker-hosted BoldTrail (kvCORE) with no SEO presence; the team runs Facebook ads and paid lead services with poor results.
- CRM: Follow Up Boss. Every form must capture what FUB requires (name, email, phone, message, source, lead type/intent, property/agent/community context, UTM/campaign).
- IDX/MLS: must be vendor-neutral; presentation, search, maps, filters, saved listings/searches and lead routing are separate from any IDX provider.
- A concept pitch to Brandon & Kait; a parallel Concept 1 ("The Quarterly", architecture-magazine editorial) already exists, so Concept 2 must be a genuinely different direction.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: home narrative with intent paths (Buying, Selling, Relocating, Exploring, Advice); property search with Search mode and Discovery mode; property detail and property stories (editorial copy clearly separated from verified data); community pages (Worcester, Shrewsbury, Grafton, Holden, Auburn, Millbury first); life-first discovery by neutral housing/location attributes only; editorial journal; team directory and per-agent pages at /agents/<slug>/; buyer journey (Explore → Save → Tour → Evaluate → Offer → Navigate → Close); seller journey (Understand → Prepare → Position → Launch → Negotiate → Close) with valuation consultation.
- Structured relationships: agent member-of team; team affiliated-with brokerage; agent ↔ listings, communities, articles. New agents come from the CMS and get a designed profile automatically.
- Lead metadata: source page, property, agent, community, intent, campaign, UTM; agent-level attribution with team-level routing rules.
- Fair-housing: no demographic, school-quality-as-proxy, or "family-friendly"-style steering; discovery uses neutral attributes only.
- WCAG 2.2 AA, Core Web Vitals, progressive enhancement, reduced-motion support.
- Undecided: final tagline, domain name, IDX vendor, final compliance wording, MLS disclosure text.

## Brand Commitments

- Consumer brand name: "The Oberdorfer Team". No existing logo.
- Strategic ideas (not automatic taglines): "Find your place." and "Real estate, personally handled."
- Brief-pinned feel: warm, knowledgeable, local, trustworthy, modern, cultured, calm, responsive, thoughtful.
- Brief-pinned refusals: corporate realtor aesthetics, generic luxury, black-and-gold, house-roof logos, keys, handshakes, stock families, glassmorphism, purple gradients, generic card grids, SaaS layouts, barns/leaves/farmhouse fonts, Inter/Poppins/Montserrat.
- Brief-pinned visual idea: "framing place" (openings, photographic framing, map boundaries, property lines, margins, spatial layers), subtle, not a literal window motif. Central MA materials (brick, slate, stone, industrial Worcester, winter/autumn light) translated into contemporary design.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: team leads Brandon Oberdorfer (MA broker license holder) and Kait Oberdorfer; brokerage REWAP Brokerage LLC, Worcester MA.
- No photography exists. The concept uses art-directed placeholder frames carrying shot-list direction, to be replaced by a real shoot.
- No listings, testimonials, sales figures, awards, years of experience, license numbers, phone numbers or emails are on hand. All listings, prices, bios and contact details in the concept are synthetic demonstration content and must be labeled as such. No statistics or claims may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. A person, not a portal: every page ends in a specific human and a specific next step.
2. Facts are sacred: verified data is visually and structurally distinct from editorial voice; nothing factual is invented.
3. Place before property: towns, streets and seasons give homes their meaning.
4. Owned and portable: the brokerage is a configurable affiliation, never the foundation.
5. Fast and fair: search works for everyone, on any device, and discovery never steers by who lives somewhere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target: keyboard paths through search, filters, galleries and dialogs; visible focus; 24px+ touch targets; reduced-motion alternatives; screen-reader semantics for map/list, favorites and form status. Fair Housing Act compliance in all discovery and copy.
